## Grounded Release Recommendation

### Final Verdict: **QUALIFIED RELEASE CERTIFIED**

Based on this comprehensive forensic audit:
1. **The methodology is sound, consistent, and strictly leakage-free**: The 106 canonical session groups guarantee complete isolation between training, validation, and testing.
2. **All audit discrepancies have been conclusively resolved**:
   - The Wilcoxon signed-rank test direction has been proven positive ($R^+ = 3544.0 > R^- = 1506.0$, $p = 0.00023$ one-sided, $p = 0.00046$ two-sided) while clarifying its minimal physical magnitude and disclaiming statistical equivalence. Both sample std ($0.1120$) and population std ($0.1115$) are reconciled.
   - Attribution provenance is verified with 100/100 cohort flows present in `test_data.npz`, exact logit identity ($0.00 \times 10^0$), and additivity residuals bounded by $7 \times 10^{-6}$.
   - Primary cluster bootstrap inference is aligned with the **16 canonical session groups** (Estimand A $-0.0778$, CI $[-0.1130, +0.3275]$; Estimand B $+0.2270$, CI $[+0.0639, +0.3562]$), with the population shift for sensitivity analysis (excluding `skype_audio3`) and the 19-file comparison transparently reported.
   - Hypothesis H4 has been formalized into three separate findings (TV class conditioning confirmed, Top-5 overlap unconditioned, prediction agreement non-estimable).
   - Hardware benchmarks cleanly disentangle median latency speedup from mean throughput speedup, tracing to a single authoritative source (`hardware_benchmarks.json`), avoiding unverified framework overhead assertions, and bounding claims away from embedded microcontrollers without physical profiling.
   - Murphy decomposition proves QAT's calibration metrics stem from Non-VPN probability shrinkage on a 94.5% majority class rather than superior intrinsic confidence calibration.
3. **The manuscript is thesis- and publication-ready**: All claims are bounded strictly to the ISCX 2016 benchmark split and empirical evidence. No further training or re-splitting is required.
